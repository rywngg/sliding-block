Sliding Block - Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. This policy covers the iPhone game Sliding Block, published by Ryan Wang.

The short version

Sliding Block has no account, and we do not collect or store personal information ourselves. The game can show you a short video ad when you choose to watch one to earn a hint. Those ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google's advertising software in the game collects some information from your device to show and measure them, as described below.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, Speed Run records, hint balance, keycap set, settings, statistics and unfinished puzzles are saved in a file inside the game's own storage on your iPhone. That file is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. If you use iCloud or device backups, your backup may include the file, as it does for any app.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

When you have used all your hints, the Get More Hints window offers "Watch Ad - Get 1 Hint". Ads are only ever shown when you tap that button; there are no banner ads and no ads between levels. The ad software is started only when you first open the Get More Hints window. From then on, Google's software in the game may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, including the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate (not precise) location;
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them (for example video views and taps);
  • performance and diagnostic information about the ad software, and basic device information such as model, operating system version and language.

Google uses this to choose, show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, and to detect fraud. How Google handles this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking. The first time you open Get More Hints, iOS asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are not personalised using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Consent and opt-out. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. Where this applies, "Ad privacy choices" in the Get More Hints window lets you change your answer at any time.
  • Not watching. Ads are always optional. You can also buy hint packs, or simply play without hints.

Purchases

Optional sound packs, premium keycap sets and hint packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. The game only learns from Apple whether a purchase is valid, so that it can unlock the sound pack or keycap set, or add the hints. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated as suitable for a general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version of the game is released.
