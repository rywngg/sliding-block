Sliding Block - Support

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a key next to the empty slot to slide it in. Tap a key further along the same row or column to slide the whole line at once. Put the numbers in order, with the empty slot in the bottom-right corner.

How do hints work? Tap Hint and the right key lights up, with an arrow toward the gap. You start with 3 free hints. When they are used up, Get More Hints lets you watch a short ad for one hint or buy a hint pack. A Speed Run that used a hint is marked "assisted" and does not replace your record.

I bought a sound pack or keycap set and it is missing. Tap Restore Purchases (in the Shop, at the bottom of the Keycaps screen, or in Settings under More) while signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Sound packs and keycap sets are one-time purchases.

Can I restore hints? No. Hint packs are used up as you play, so like other consumable purchases they cannot be restored on another device or after deleting the game.

How do I get more keycap sets? Tap Keycaps on the first screen. Five sets are yours from the start, seven more unlock as you complete Classic levels (the Keycaps screen shows how many levels the next one needs), and four premium sets are optional purchases. Statistics are in Settings, under More.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Tracking: iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Consent (where the law requires it): "Ad privacy choices" in the Get More Hints window.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
